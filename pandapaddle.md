# PandaPaddle
Pickleball paddles are expensive and way out of budget for people just starting out. The goal of this project is to design a 3D printable pickleball
paddle that delivers comparable performance to leading consumer models at less than $10 in materials costs.

Goals:  
Fully USA Pickleball paddle regulations compliant  
Comparable performance to typical beginner/intermediate level paddles  
<$10 in materials costs  

Tools Used:  
SolidWorks  
Bambu Lab X1E/H2D/H2D Pro printers
